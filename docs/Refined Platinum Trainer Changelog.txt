*Revised all of the boss trainers along with a few that weren’t touched before such as Fight Area’s Flint and Volkner fight.
*All of the changes before beating Cynthia are not present in the main patch, while changes concerning postgame trainers are in both.
v4.1.1
*Undid trainer level reductions originally meant to compensate for buffs to bosses.
*Slightly nerfed endgame trainer levels so the player is less likely to overlevel.
*Improved documentation considerably
*Gym Leaders, E4, etc now have their ace as the last Pokemon for consistency.
*Minor tweaks.
v4.0.5
*Slight rebalancing to lategame exp curve.
*Improved Lopunny/Klutz interactions in builds.
*Gave some random Tuber an Aqua Tail Azumarill instead of Bubblebeam.
v4.0
*Superbosses have been reworked. THE FRONTIER BRAINS HAVE BEEN ADDED, and progression has changed; the player must now beat either the Aide or Post-Stark Elite Four to take on the Frontier and Weather superbosses.
**The Superboss progression is largely nonlinear, with the only gates being Stark Mountain for the first set, Aide for the rest, plus specifically the Weather bosses for the final superboss.
**Rematch Elite Four, Battlegrounds, Frontier Brains, and so on are all not required stepping stones but rather various exemplars of a grand pantheon.
*Various rematches have been buffed. There should be few Post-Stark rematches with only 1 Pokemon.
*Buck and Marley have been slightly nerfed as partners.


v3.2
*Buffed Post-Cyrus trainers, as well as Byron and Wake.
*Nerfed Battle Zone levels to start at mid-50s and end at 60.
*Nerfed the Weather Superbosses slightly.
v3.1.2
*Minor IV changes.
v3.1
*Substantial improvements to the postgame as a whole, as well as Pokemon Center fights, and modest buffs for the Snowpoint-League stretch of the game.
*Some rematches have been given level and team buffs, but no extra IVs.
*The vast majority of main game teams have not been edited.
*Volkner's Rotom is now Rotom-Fan. It still acts like a base Rotom.

2a About Trainers
Ultimately, one of the few gripes I have with Platinum is that there isn't too much to do in the postgame. While the Battle Zone is visually interesting, with fascinating new biomes and a sorely-needed tropical tileset, it lacks any particular oomph to it. Furthermore, after Stark, there isn't really anything worth your while. Barry requires literally 20 rematches with the Elite Four to get the final version of him, and both the Battleground and Elite Four Rematch teams are...underwhelming.
So, why not have more content. As part of following up on the Galactic Commanders, each of them can be fought, and they have a real punch to them. The Battlegrounds, Elite Four, and so on are all revamped and improved. And, of course, the endgame and postgame have more bite than before, if not THAT much.


Of course, at the end of the day, this is a 493 patch, not a difficulty hack. However, this should make it so all of that exp can be done in ways that test your mettle instead of cycling the same two trainers.


In v4.0, I got a bit over the top about all of it and threw in the Frontier Brains on top of the preexisting set. To compensate, they're now a bit gated.
These are balanced around first making the Stark Mountain E4 rematches closer together, and then designing Superbosses as being post-Rematch Cynthia.
The design philosophy is to have three close together tiers of superbosses, as can be seen in 1c, and to give the player a pantheon of challenges to chose from instead of adding more progression.


Movesets are MOSTLY legal, with a few exceptions. All of the individual parts are legal, at any rate. I have tried to keep things consistent within a set of rematches, but there may be small anomalies. Any disparities can be explained by the other trainers having access to a magical move tutor that can teach any legal move, who lives upstairs in that house in Sootopolis.
Seriously, I tried my best. Stuff slips through the cracks.


2b IV Curve
Battle Zone trainers have around 12 IVs in normal fights with 18 IVs in rematches. To compensate, Partners Buck and Marley have higher IVs now so they can stand up to the stronger foes. Note that a 0 IV Pokemon with 252 EVs in a stat will generally be stronger than a 31 IV Pokemon with 0 EVs in said stat.
Late-game trainers have also been given a small amount of IVs instead of literally 0 every time. This should modestly improve difficulty without requiring EV training or IV breeding. Precise IV values may vary since having a given amount of IVs is equal to just being a level higher, but by Sunyshore are generally at least 4.

2e Ability Changes
This is strictly just a list of Pokemon who have had their first and second abilities swapped so trainers will make more efficient use of their Pokemon. Trainers in Platinum use the FIRST ability by default.
This does not affect gameplay in any way shape or form outside of which abilities trainers use. Battle Frontier abilities are randomly picked, and so are unaffected.
In the future, this will be restored to match HGSS’s ability selection, thus making trainers have case-dependent abilities instead of blanket changes like this. The changes are as they are to directly enable various trainers whose teams would otherwise be handicapped due to a lack of a vital ability.
