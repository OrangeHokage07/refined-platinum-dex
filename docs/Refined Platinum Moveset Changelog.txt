1b About
Instead of altering an entire Pokemon’s purpose, this should mostly let you fine tune some builds or lower the barrier to entry of using a mon without TMs.
All of these are moves the Pokemon can legally know in this generation (as of HGSS), only changed to make it less problematic to get them. Includes things like fixing pre-evolution only moves, updating learnsets so Pokemon can get meaningful STAB, and giving the Dratini line Extremespeed to match the HGSS event.
The most problematic Pokemon such as Shieldon and Cherubi have also been given extra moves from their Tutor or Egg learnsets, giving them actual STAB options and the like. This should generally feel like straight QoL instead of new movepools, without taking significantly away from the resource management inherent in Gen 4 TMs.


As an added bonus, all stone evolutions now have a barebones level up pool for more flexibility - though I still recommend keeping that Clefairy unevolved until midgame.


It isn't the most dramatic changelog, but Pokemon like Shieldon and Stunky should be much easier to use this way.

4X Legendary Move Changes


Legendary level up moves have been changed significantly to make them into worthwhile fights at the levels they are fought. They still can't hold a candle to even basic trainer AI, but hopefully it's more exciting.
All movesets are still legal in Gen 4. Yes, this means no Sacred Fire Entei, I'm sorry.
Events are not included as legal move options with the exception of Arceus's Spacial Rend.
Dialga, Palkia and Giratina have Power Gem/Shadow Sneak at 65, Slash at 75, and Metal Burst/Hydro Pump/Destiny Bond at 85 respectively.


Note that most post-Dex legends are level 65 while most post-Stark legends are level 80.
The movesets used for the battle are learned one level below the level it is fought at.
