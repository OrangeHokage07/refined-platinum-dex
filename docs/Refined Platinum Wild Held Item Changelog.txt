1b About
TMs were not added to held items.
A Pokemon being mentioned here does not mean it is encounterable in the wild. Items are usually set up to be held by evolution lines. Depending on how easy it is to encounter the Pokemon in either HGSS OR Platinum, evolved forms may or may not have a 50%.
Most resistance berries are held by Pokemon who can make use of them, with a few exceptions where appropriate. (Mareep has fluffy fur that would get Colbur stuck in them, Gyarados is not a dragon but gets Haban because it's so funny)


100% here means both slots are filled by the same item.
From what I can tell, the game does one check at the beginning of battle, so having a 50% and a 5% gives an about 55% chance of an item with 50% of item X and 5% of item Y. In other words, adding a 50% chance item does not appear to lower the chance of a 5% one.


These are all ADDITIONS; some things were not needed - for example, Chople Berry is available off of Buneary in Sinnoh.

Most legendaries now have items to give them a bit more oomph. These are largely offensive items, usually to boost their main STAB or give them their special item. Deoxys acknowledges that it's fragile as hell, and is particularly nasty in how it executes it.
To the best of my knowledge, Roaming Pokemon will never hold an item, but I'm NOT 100% certain of this.


Latias and Latios probably SHOULD have Soul Dew, but since it's event exclusive at best, nullified in the Battle Frontier and banned basically everywhere, it has been omitted.
